# Epilepsy trigger model

## Evidence-backed visual risks
The Epilepsy Foundation identifies flashing lights and visual patterns as potential seizure triggers for photosensitive users. Common provocative flash frequencies are about 5–30 Hz, but individual sensitivity varies. Brightness, contrast, wavelength, distance and other conditions affect risk.

The engineering response is therefore to reduce exposure and provide user-controlled conservative profiles rather than declare a single universally safe threshold.

## Included categories
- Flicker/strobing
- Rapid visual transitions
- High-contrast stripes/checkerboards/patterns
- Brightness and contrast
- Motion/animation
- Video/game content
- Individual sound sensitivity
- Individual vibration/haptic sensitivity

## Non-claims
The application cannot guarantee prevention of seizures. It cannot universally intercept every application's raw video, audio or haptic output on a normal non-root Android device.
